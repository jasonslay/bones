How to play
  • 1s score 100 · 5s score 50
  • Three 1s = 1000 · five 1s = 2000
  • Three of a kind = face × 100 · four of a kind = face × 1000
  • Five of a kind (2–6) wins instantly
  • Get 1,000 in one turn to get on the board
  • First to exactly 10,000 wins
  • On the board? You can steal leftover dice from the player before you
